# Raster publication test

These evaluation projects display CNRFC and UCRB forcing and land-state images
from producer-published metadata. Existing operational raster families are unchanged.

Variables, colors, image placement, and available dates come from the publication.
Daily dates label the start of a UTC interval; monthly dates identify calendar
months. The producer's preferred timeline selects among available streams.
Transparent images may represent valid conditions with no values highlighted.

These publications are for evaluation, not operational use or redistribution.
P denotes precipitation, T temperature, SM column soil water (upper 2 m), and
SWE snow water equivalent. %-ile denotes non-exceedance percentile rank.
These images use existing model snapshots; recent forcing revisions may not be
reflected in model states. This is not a new operational forecast product.

Boundary and river overlays provide geographic context. Legacy forecast-point
popups are not included in these test projects.
